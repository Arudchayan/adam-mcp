Every Uni Basel student lives in ADAM. Finding what changed shouldn't take twenty clicks.

Today: dig through courses, folders, and pages. Deadlines hide. News is easy to miss.

So I built adam-mcp — ADAM, for the AI tools you already use.

Ask what changed. Pull every deadline. Search the reading. Open the page. Same ADAM — seconds, not a scavenger hunt.

Runs on your machine. Uses your Chrome session. No cloud tunnel of your SWITCH login.

Open source. github.com/Arudchayan/adam-mcp.
