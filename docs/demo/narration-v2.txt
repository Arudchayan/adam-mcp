ADAM MCP connects your University of Basel courses to an MCP-compatible assistant. Here is one study workflow, illustrated with synthetic course data.

Sign in through a dedicated Chrome session. Ask for your courses, then choose the one you want to explore. Course links take you back to ADAM.

Ask for available deadlines in that course. Results cite their source. If a listing is incomplete, the assistant should say so. An empty folder does not mean there are no deadlines.

Search your enrolled course tree for a topic, such as Fourier transforms. Search covers titles and available page text. It is not ADAM's global search.

Select a page and approve the read. Page text, file extracts, exercise instructions, and forum posts require approval for the selected object. Declining returns no body text.

The assistant can work with the approved text and cite its ADAM link. Extracts are bounded. Scanned PDFs have no OCR, and mathematical notation may need checking in the original.

The server runs on your machine. Sign-in stays in Chrome. Returned text goes to the model provider chosen in your client. Exams, grades, submissions, and other writes are unsupported.

Explore the setup guide and client compatibility notes in the repository. ADAM MCP is an unofficial, open-source community project. This illustration does not certify a client integration.
