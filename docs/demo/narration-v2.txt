ADAM MCP helps University of Basel students find and read their course materials.

Course news, folders, pages, and deadlines are available through your MCP client.

Connect your ADAM courses to an MCP-compatible assistant.

Read course news. Find available deadlines. Search course materials. Read an approved page. Results include ADAM links and report when coverage is incomplete.

Runs locally with a dedicated Chrome session. Returned text goes to your chosen model provider.

Open source. github.com/Arudchayan/adam-mcp.
