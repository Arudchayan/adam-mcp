## Installed-client walkthroughs

Three independent Claude chats used only the `adam` MCP tools against the installed release-candidate tarball. They reported counts and error codes only, with no course content.

1. **First run** (release commit `1bb98c8`). 8 of 9 steps passed. Two problems surfaced:
   - Unscoped `adam_list_calendar` and `adam_list_news` timed out in the client (4 of 4 calls).
   - Mail headers kept the ILIAS column labels, and `hasAttachments` was always true.

   Smaller findings: undecoded `&amp;` in news text and one duplicated exercise deadline. All four were fixed in #167 (ADR 0061).
2. **Second run** (`95f8de3`). Everything passed, except that the first parallel unscoped calendar and news calls timed out once and succeeded on retry. The client limit is about 30 s. The walk budget was lowered to 25 s in #168 (ADR 0061 amendment).
3. **Third run** (`ef4d314`, the published bytes). All nine steps passed:
   - Status was signed in before and after.
   - 8 courses listed.
   - The course snapshot and children agreed.
   - A read without approval returned `confirmation_required` with no text, and the approved read returned `untrusted: true` text.
   - Unscoped calendar (17) and news (20) returned without a timeout and not partial, with no entities and no duplicate deadline.
   - Mail returned 22 headers, 8 unread, 0 attachments and no label prefixes.
   - `adam_list_course_members` returned `forbidden`.

Explicit logout was not repeated, so the student's session was not ended. The CLI logout path is unchanged since the [12 November record](2026-11-12-v0.4.0-release-readiness.md), where it was exercised.
