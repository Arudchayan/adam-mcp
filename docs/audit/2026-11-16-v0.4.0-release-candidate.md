## Known limits carried into 0.4.0

- The staff views are **lab-verified, ADAM-unverified** (ADR 0060). They fail closed (`provider_unavailable`) on markup drift and return `forbidden` to students.
- The new ILIAS object types are identity, citation and page text only. Deep readers wait on #165.
- The Private Notes item markup is unverified (ADR 0056). iCal export is out of scope.
