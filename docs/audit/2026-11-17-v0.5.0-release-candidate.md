## Known limits carried into 0.5.0

- Lab-verified readers may differ from ADAM's markup. They fall back to page text (`detailStatus: "unrecognised"`).
- Not covered (ADR 0062 amendment):
  - booking weeks other than the current one
  - data-collection paging
  - mediacast descriptions of items other than the one shown
  - content-page sections other than headings
  - HTML-module files other than the start file
  - session materials under a group parent
- Still not possible in the lab: Opencast and Etherpad (UniBasel plugins), and the staff views on ADAM (needs a tutor account). These stay in #165.
